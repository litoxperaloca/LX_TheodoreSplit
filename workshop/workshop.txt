description=[h1]LX Theodore Split-Screen Fix[/h1]
description=Splitscreen / split screen / local couch co-op fix for the Theodore mod in Project Zomboid Build 42 (B42), plus the gamepad support it never had. Your son finally notices all 2, 3 or 4 of you.
description=Theodore is a lovely mod that says, right in its own description, single-player only. It does not have to be.
description=
description=[h2]What it does[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]He sees every local player[/b] - Theodore asks the game for "the player" 166 times, and outside single-player that call only ever returns one of you.
description=[*][b]Your saves keep working[/b] - Two things must NOT move between players: where his saved data lives, and whether he calls you Mom or Dad.
description=[*][b]His menus open for whoever asked[/b] - Theodore built his context menus for player 0, hardcoded. Player 2 could not open them at all - with a mouse or a gamepad.
description=[*][b]His panel stays on your half of the screen, and takes a gamepad[/b] - Theodore's panel and floating button were placed against the whole window, so in split-screen they drifted onto the other player's half.
description=[*][b]One son, not one per player[/b] - Theodore stays a single character the household shares, which is how the original models him.
description=[*][b]Nothing of Theodore is redistributed[/b] - His files are read from your installed copy at load time, patched in memory and run from there.
description=[/list]
description=[b]Requires:[/b] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3830500541]Theodore[/url]
description=[b]Load it AFTER[/b] Theodore.
description=[b]Install:[/b] Subscribe, then enable it under Mods in the main menu. In split-screen, enabling it once covers every player.
