title=LX Theodore Split-Screen Fix
description=[h1]LX Theodore Split-Screen Fix[/h1]
description=[i]Your son notices every player, and works with a controller[/i]
description=Theodore is a lovely mod that says, right in its own description, single-player only. It does not have to be.
description=Split-screen is the mode that carries the most bugs in stable B42 - and the mods worth playing with, like Bandits and Project A-Life, are built for singleplayer and online multiplayer. Nobody is fixing couch co-op.
description=
description=[h2]What it does[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]He sees every local player[/b] - Theodore asks the game for "the player" 166 times, and outside single-player that call only ever returns one of you.
description=[*][b]Your saves keep working[/b] - Two things must NOT move between players: where his saved data lives, and whether he calls you Mom or Dad. Those stay anchored to player 1, exactly where the original put them, so a save from before this mod loads unchanged.
description=[*][b]His menus open for whoever asked[/b] - Theodore built his context menus for player 0, hardcoded. Player 2 could not open them at all - with a mouse or a gamepad.
description=[*][b]One son, not one per player[/b] - Theodore stays a single character the household shares, which is how the original models him. Both of you feed him, talk to him and keep him safe; he just answers to whoever is nearby.
description=[*][b]Nothing of Theodore is redistributed[/b] - His files are read from your installed copy at load time, patched in memory and run from there. The mod always sits on top of whatever Theodore version you have, and none of his work is shipped inside this mod.
description=[/list]

description=[b]Load it AFTER[/b] Theodore.
description=[b]Install:[/b] Subscribe, then enable it under Mods in the main menu. In split-screen, enabling it once covers every player.
